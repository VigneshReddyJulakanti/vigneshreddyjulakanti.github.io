Privacy & account deletion

Learn how Loop Puzzle saves your progress, or request deletion of your game account and cloud save.

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Privacy policy

This policy covers Loop Puzzle: Tap Puzzle Game (shown in the app as Loop Puzzle), the Android game, package com.vignesh.orbitsweep, and this privacy and deletion page. For privacy questions, contact the Loop Puzzle developer at hindisongslife@gmail.com.

Playing without an account

Google sign-in is optional. Without signing in, game progress is saved on your device. Local data includes completed levels, stars, fastest completion times, an unfinished board, game settings, and hint and life balances.

Optional daily reminders

If you enable daily reminders, the app keeps recent play times, app-opening dates, and reminder delivery dates on this device to choose a suitable time and limit reminders to one per day when you have not opened the app. These records are not uploaded to our cloud service. You can turn reminders off in Settings or change notification permission in Android settings. Clearing the app's local storage removes this reminder history.

Google sign-in and cloud saves

If you choose Google sign-in, we use Google Firebase Authentication to identify your game account. The app uses your Firebase account identifier and Google account email address. Google handles your Google password; the game does not receive it.

Cloud Firestore stores your completed levels and progression, best stars per level, and fastest completion time per level under your account identifier. This lets you back up progress, restore it on another device, and merge progress when linking your account.

Progress is saved locally after each completion. Routine cloud uploads are batched after two completed level runs; explicit sign-in or linking can merge progress immediately. Unsynced progress may be lost if local app data is removed.

Services and information sharing

We use Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, and Cloud Firestore to provide account access and cloud saves. These services process information needed to operate and secure those features under Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not sell your account information or saved progress.

Advertising and consent

The game is intended for a mixed-age audience. Before online advertising starts, we ask for an age range, with a “Prefer not to say” option. Only that selection is stored on this device; we do not collect a birth date or send the age range to our cloud-save service. Under-18 and unknown-age selections receive child-directed and under-age-of-consent advertising treatment, with ad content rated for general audiences. Optional Google sign-in and cloud saves are not initialized for those selections; offline play remains available.

The game includes Google AdMob for banner ads and optional rewarded ads, such as ads that provide a hint or heart. When ads are enabled, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares information including IP addresses, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers. This information supports advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. IP addresses can be used to estimate a device’s general location. See Google’s advertising data disclosures.

The game uses Google’s consent tools to display privacy choices when required. Where available, you can revisit these choices through the game’s ad privacy options. Ad behavior depends on your consent choices, region, device settings, and the game’s audience configuration. Playing without a game account does not prevent ad services from processing information when ads are enabled.

This page and support email

If you send that email, your email address, request, and any details you include will be received at our Gmail support address and used to handle your request. The provider hosting this page may process ordinary connection information, such as an IP address, to serve the page.

Retention and your choices

Cloud account data and saved progress are kept until deleted. You can request deletion using the instructions below. Request-related email may be retained as needed to handle the request and keep a record of its resolution. If any data must be retained for legal or security reasons, we will explain this when responding to your request.

Google controls retention of information it processes independently for its services, including advertising, under its own privacy policies. Deleting your Loop Puzzle game account does not delete your Google account or independently held Google advertising data.

Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete your cloud account. Device-local progress can be removed through Android’s app storage settings. You can contact us to ask about your data or request a correction.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Account & data requests

Request account deletion

You can request deletion without installing or opening the game. Email hindisongslife@gmail.com from the Google email address linked to your Loop Puzzle account, or use the form below to prepare your message.

  1. Include your linked Google email address and state that you want your Loop Puzzle account and cloud progress deleted.
  2. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request. Never send your password, sign-in codes, or payment details.
  3. We will respond by email about your request and confirm when deletion is complete. Preparing or sending a request does not instantly delete your account.

What will be deleted: your Loop Puzzle Firebase Authentication account and its Firestore cloud save, including level progress, stars, and fastest times.

This does not delete your Google account or data in other apps. Cloud deletion does not remotely erase local saves on your devices. Clear Loop Puzzle’s local app storage if you also want to remove those saves; signing in again can create a new game account and upload remaining local progress.

Send the request from this same address so we can verify ownership.

You will review the email and press Send in your email app. This page cannot send email automatically.

Open email app

If no email app opens, copy the request into Gmail or another email service and send it to hindisongslife@gmail.com.

Need help?

For privacy, account deletion, or saved-progress questions, contact hindisongslife@gmail.com. Include “Loop Puzzle” in the subject so we can identify the game.
